Sponsor

Partner with WHEAT

Local businesses and organizations can sponsor a season or help cover a specific need — robot parts, travel, or shop tools. Sponsorship supports students directly and puts your name in front of an engaged community of families, educators, and future engineers. Team 41 has been supported by sponsors including Picatinny Arsenal, Nokia Bell Labs, Petillo Companies, the Warren Lions Club, the Gene Haas Foundation, Thorlabs, The Knotts Company, and the Intuitive Foundation.
