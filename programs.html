Our Programs

WHEAT exists to fund and support Team 41, the RoboWarriors, and the outreach work the team runs throughout the year.

Grades 9–12 · FIRST Robotics Competition

Team 41 — RoboWarriors

A student-led team that designs, builds, programs, and competes with a robot each season in the FIRST Mid-Atlantic District, developing real-world skills in CAD, mechanical design, electronics, software, and project management. Visit team41robo.com or follow @team41robotics on Instagram.

  • Meets: Watchung Hills Regional High School, Warren, NJ (Room 4)
  • Open to: WHRHS students from Warren, Watchung, Green Brook, and Long Hill
  • Team size: 40+ students, 3 faculty advisors, 4 industry mentors
  • Funded by WHEAT: robot parts, maintenance, registration fees, travel
Build season · Jan–Mar

Six-Week Build Season

Each January, Team 41 receives a new game challenge from FIRST and has six weeks to design, manufacture, and test a competition robot — using the team's own CNC machine and plasma cutter (acquired 2021) — before shipping it to competition.

  • Format: intensive after-school and weekend build sessions
  • Open to: all current Team 41 members
  • Funded by WHEAT: materials, tools, and meals during build season
All ages · Mentorship

Mentor & Advisor Network

Faculty advisors and volunteer mentors from the local engineering industry coach the team, lead sub-teams, and support students one-on-one throughout the year.

  • Current advisors: Daniel Wasserman, Jay Stiles, Mark Mariak
  • Sponsors & partners: Picatinny Arsenal, Nokia Bell Labs, Petillo Companies, Thorlabs, Gene Haas Foundation, Warren Lions Club, and others
  • Funded by WHEAT: training and materials
Community · Outreach

Robo Guide

Team 41 members run community outreach including the Robo Guide program and presentations at the WHRHS Academic Fair, introducing younger students across the district to robotics and engineering.

  • Open to: families and students across the Watchung Hills district
  • Cost: free, thanks to WHEAT donors
  • Funded by WHEAT: materials and event support
