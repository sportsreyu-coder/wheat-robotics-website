About WHEAT

The Watchung Hills Education and Technology Foundation is the all-volunteer, parent-run nonprofit behind Team 41, the RoboWarriors — the FIRST Robotics Competition team at Watchung Hills Regional High School in Warren, NJ.

Our Mission

Mission

To support Watchung Hills Regional High School students in programs designed to inspire them to pursue education and career opportunities in science, technology, engineering, and math — while building self-confidence, knowledge, and life skills.

What We Do

Our Role

WHEAT raises funds for Team 41's robot parts, maintenance, registration, and travel, and provides the organizational and logistics support — ordering supplies, coordinating volunteers, feeding students — that keeps a student-run robotics team running through the six-week build season.

What We Believe

Our Values

Access

Cost and equipment should never be the reason a student can't participate in STEM.

Mentorship

Students learn best alongside adults and peers who guide, challenge, and believe in them.

Community

Our programs are built by and for the Watchung Hills community, in partnership with local schools.

Our Story

History

Team 41 predates WHEAT by over a decade — the foundation was formed to put the team's fundraising and finances on a formal, nonprofit footing.

  • 1997 — Team 41 founded

    The RoboWarriors fielded their first robot, winning the Judges' Award at the Johnson & Johnson Mid-Atlantic Regional in their rookie FIRST Robotics Competition season.

  • 2008 — Parents organize to fund the team

    As the program grew, parent volunteers began formally organizing fundraising and logistics support for the team each build season.

  • 2010 — WHEAT incorporated as a 501(c)(3)

    The Watchung Hills Education and Technology Foundation was formally incorporated (EIN 26-3031711) to raise funds and manage finances on the team's behalf.

  • Today — 28 seasons strong

    Team 41 has competed in 28 seasons, attended 60 events, won 21 awards, and traveled to the FRC World Championship 8 times — all made possible by WHEAT's fundraising and volunteer support.
