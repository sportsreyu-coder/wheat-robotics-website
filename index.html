Building pathways into STEM, one student at a time.

WHEAT — the Watchung Hills Education and Technology Foundation — is the all-volunteer, parent-run nonprofit behind the RoboWarriors, FIRST Robotics Competition Team 41 at Watchung Hills Regional High School. Since 2008 we've raised funds and provided logistics support so students from Warren, Watchung, Green Brook, and Long Hill can design, build, and compete with robots.

I · What We Do

Three ways WHEAT invests in students

WHEAT is run entirely by volunteers — mostly parents of RoboWarriors team members — and exists to raise funds and provide organizational and logistics support for Team 41, especially during the critical six-week build season.

Fund the Team

We raise and manage funds that cover robot parts and maintenance, registration fees, and travel to regional and district competitions for Team 41.

Support Build Season

We provide the organizational and logistics backbone — ordering parts, coordinating volunteers, feeding students — that keeps a student-run team running during the six-week build season.

Expand Access

We work to lower the barriers that keep students out of STEM — cost, equipment, and exposure — so every student at Watchung Hills who is curious gets a chance to build, code, and compete.

II · Our Programs

Programs supported by WHEAT

WHEAT is the nonprofit umbrella behind Team 41 and its outreach work. Visit the Programs page for more detail.

Robotics

Team 41 — RoboWarriors

Since 1997, students at Watchung Hills Regional High School have designed, built, and programmed a competition robot each six-week build season, competing in the FIRST Mid-Atlantic District. Learn more at team41robo.com.

Outreach

Robo Guide

Team 41 members run the Robo Guide program and present at the WHRHS Academic Fair to introduce younger students to robotics and engineering.

Mentorship

Mentor & Advisor Network

Around 40 students work alongside faculty advisors and volunteer mentors from the local engineering industry, gaining hands-on experience in CAD, manufacturing, electronics, and programming.
