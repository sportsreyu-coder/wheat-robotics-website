What We Do

Three ways WHEAT invests in students

As the parent foundation, WHEAT raises funds, coordinates volunteers, and provides organizational support for the educational and technology programs it sponsors.

Fund Programs

We raise and manage funds that cover equipment, competition fees, curriculum materials, and travel for student teams and classrooms that couldn't otherwise afford them.

Connect Mentors

We recruit and support volunteer coaches, mentors, and industry professionals who share their time and expertise with students year-round.

Expand Access

We work to lower the barriers that keep students out of STEM — cost, equipment, and exposure — so every student who is curious gets a chance to build, code, and compete.
